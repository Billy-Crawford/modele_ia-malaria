🩺 SCENARIO 1 — Rural Health Center (Most Important Use Case)

📍 Context
• Isolated health center
• No biologist on-site
• Manual microscope
• Basic Android device (Samsung Note 9 = perfect)

👩🏽‍⚕️ User
Community Health Worker

🔄 Real Workflow
1. Blood sample collection
2. Blood smear preparation
3. Photo capture via smartphone + microscope
4. AI analyzes image offline
5. Result:
   ◦ 🔴 "Infected cell – high probability"
   ◦ 🟢 "Healthy cell"

🧠 Human Decision
• If 🔴 → refer patient / confirm with rapid diagnostic test
• If 🟢 → monitoring or alternative diagnosis

💡 Real Value
✔ Reduced turnaround time
✔ Fewer human errors
✔ Assistance where no specialist is available


🧪 SCENARIO 2 — Overloaded Laboratory (Productivity Gain)

📍 Context

🔄 Usage

2. Classification:
   ◦ Suspected cases
   ◦ Likely healthy cases
3. Biologist confirms positive cases

🎯 Impact

✔ Fewer false negatives due to fatigue
✔ Standardization of results
